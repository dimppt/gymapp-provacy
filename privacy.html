Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Alea ("the app") is developed and maintained by Alea Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Alea does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any identifying information, and does not transmit any data to external servers.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your daily roll history (exercise name, rep count, timestamp)
  • Your streak and lifetime statistics (roll count, total reps)
  • Your app preferences (theme, die configuration, reminder setting)
  • Your last completed session and any pending duel challenges
  • An optional display name you enter for duel mode (self-chosen, stored only on your device)

This data is stored using your device's local storage (AsyncStorage) and never leaves your device. It is not synced to any cloud service, not shared with Alea Labs, and not accessible to any third party.

Duel Mode & Share Links

Duel challenges and shared results travel inside the link itself — the exercises, rep counts, times, and the display name you chose are encoded directly in the URL you share. Nothing is uploaded to or stored on any server; a duel exists only in the links exchanged between you and the person you challenge. Only share a duel link with people you want to see its contents. Result cards you share are images generated on your device.

Third-Party Services

Alea does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. No SDKs that transmit data externally are included.

Permissions

If you enable the optional daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device — no push servers are involved. The app requests no other device permissions (camera, location, microphone, contacts, etc.).

Children's Privacy

Alea does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no personal data from any user, it is safe for use by all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
