Ferro by Alea Labs

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The short version: Ferro keeps your habit data on your device. We don't run accounts or servers for it, we can't see what you track, and we don't sell your data. The only information that leaves your device is what's needed to process a subscription, handled by trusted providers.

Who we are

Ferro ("the app") is a personal discipline and habit-tracking app published by Alea Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and your rights over it.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Ferro — your rules and protocols, daily check-ins, streaks, journal entries, rest days, recovery tokens, reminders, and settings — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no ability to access or read it.

If you delete the app or clear its data, this information is permanently removed from your device.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email, name, or login is required to use Ferro.
  • We do not run analytics on the contents of your habits or journal.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers.

Third-party services

To offer optional paid features (Ferro Pro), the app uses the following providers. Each processes only what is necessary and under its own privacy policy:

  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and purchases. It processes purchase records and an anonymous app-generated identifier and device information to determine your subscription status. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process your payment and manage billing when you subscribe. Your payment details are handled by the store, never by us. See Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.

Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device; they are not sent through any server and involve no personal data leaving your device.

Legal basis and your rights

Because your habit data never leaves your device, you remain in full control of it at all times. Depending on your location (including under the EU/EEA GDPR), you have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data. For on-device data, you can exercise these directly by viewing, editing, or clearing the app's data. For subscription data held by our providers, contact us and we will assist.

Children

Ferro is not directed to children under the age required by your local law (13, or 16 in parts of the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
