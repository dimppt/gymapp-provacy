Questions

The short answers.

Is Alea free?

Yes. Everything the app does today is free and stays free: the roll, the streak, duels, sessions, all of it. There are no ads and there never will be, and nothing expires on a trial clock.

Do I need any equipment?

Almost none. Everything is bodyweight: the floor, a wall and you. A few pull movements (pull-ups, inverted rows, muscle-ups) want a bar or a sturdy edge. Everything else needs nothing at all.

Do I need an account?

No. Using Alea needs no account: no sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and roll. Even duels work without one, because the challenge travels inside the link.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Alea runs entirely on your phone and keeps your history in local storage, so it works with no signal at all.

What exercises are in it?

Twenty bodyweight movements across push, pull, legs, core, cardio, plyometrics and balance, from push-ups, squats and burpees to pistol squats, L-sit holds and muscle-ups. Your dice size decides how many of them are in play.

Is it for beginners or advanced?

Both, and you set which. A small exercise die keeps you on the fundamentals with gentle rep counts; scale it up and the pool grows into the hard stuff with rep values up to 75.

How do duels work?

Finish a workout against the clock and share the challenge card. Whoever opens it gets your exact roll and races the same clock. The faster time wins, and the result card settles it. Challenges expire after 48 hours. They don't need the app installed to open your challenge, and neither of you needs an account.

What if I have nobody to duel?

Race yourself. Alea banks your last twelve timed sessions, so your own past workouts become opponents. Or take on one of four pace-setters, from The Warm-Up to The Machine. Those are designed benchmarks, not other people's data, and solo duels never leave your phone.

What happens if I miss a day?

You get one streak freeze per calendar month, and it covers a single missed day automatically, so the chain holds. Freezes don't stack, so it's insurance against one slip rather than a pile of skip days. Miss two days in a row with no freeze left and the streak restarts at one.

Will it nag me?

Only if you want it to. The daily reminder is one notification, off unless you turn it on. Once it has seen a few of your workouts it moves to about an hour before your usual training time; before that it just asks at 6pm.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. Dark, light or follow your system theme, four accent colours, and soft or sharp corners. You can also resize both dice: the exercise die runs D4 to D20, the rep die D4 to D12.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Alea is on Android today, via Google Play.

What data does Alea collect?

Your workout history stays on your device. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
