Alea draws from twenty bodyweight movements. Each has a guide covering setup, execution, the mistakes people actually make, and how to scale it up or down.

By movement pattern

Sixteen of the twenty need no equipment at all. The four that want a bar or a sturdy edge are marked below.

Push

  • Push-ups — no equipment
  • Dips — parallel bars, or two sturdy chairs
  • Pike Push-ups — no equipment
  • Diamond Push-ups — no equipment
  • Tricep Dips — a bench, chair or step
  • Archer Push-ups — no equipment

Pull

  • Pull-ups — a pull-up bar
  • Inverted Rows — a bar at waist height, or a sturdy table
  • Muscle-ups — a pull-up bar with clearance above it

Legs

  • Squats — no equipment
  • Lunges — no equipment
  • Pistol Squats — no equipment
  • Bulgarian Split Squat — a bench, chair or sofa

Core

  • Mountain Climbers — no equipment
  • Plank Hold — timed hold, no equipment
  • L-Sit Hold — timed hold, parallettes, low bars, or the floor

Full Body

  • Burpees — no equipment

Cardio

  • Jumping Jacks — no equipment

Plyometric

  • Box Jumps — a stable box, bench or low wall

Balance

  • Handstand Hold — timed hold, a clear wall

Common questions

Do I need equipment for these?

Sixteen of the twenty need nothing at all. Pull-ups, muscle-ups and inverted rows want a bar or sturdy edge, dips are easiest on parallel bars, box jumps need something stable to jump onto, and the Bulgarian split squat wants a bench.

Why only twenty exercises?

Because a die needs a bounded, ordered pool to be meaningful. A thousand-exercise library is a thousand decisions, which is the problem Alea exists to remove.

What does effort weight mean?

It is the per-rep workload the app assigns each movement when it builds 100-rep sessions and tailored workouts. Muscle-ups are 3.0, jumping jacks 0.7, so a session heavy on muscle-ups carries far fewer total reps.

Can I choose which exercises come up?

Indirectly. The pool is ordered from foundational to advanced and the die slices from the top, so a smaller exercise die keeps you in the easier movements.
